# SnapSafe AI — 90-Second Competition Demo Script
### Snapdragon AI Lab Build & Present Challenge
**Presenter:** Project Lead / Presenter  
**Total Target Duration:** 90 Seconds  

---

### [00:00 – 00:15] The Hook & The Problem
*(Presenter stands before the judges with the HP Snapdragon AI PC running SnapSafe AI Dashboard.)*

> **Presenter:**  
> "Every single day, millions of students, engineers, and professionals share their screens during remote interviews, online classes, and client demos.  
> But here’s the silent danger: we share our screens without realizing how much sensitive information is sitting in open tabs, notes, and terminals."

---

### [00:15 – 00:30] Launching the Scenario
*(Presenter clicks **[ TRY DEMO ]** on the Dashboard.)*

> **Presenter:**  
> "Take a look at this realistic university portal screen.  
> Right here in plain view, we have a student’s full name, private email, personal phone number, student ID number, a confidential scholarship remark, and a live private API key.  
> If I share this right now over Zoom or Teams, that information is permanently compromised."

---

### [00:30 – 00:50] The Local AI Scan
*(Presenter clicks **[ SCAN SCREEN NOW ]**. The visual pipeline displays: Capturing → Detecting text → Classifying → Risk scoring.)*

> **Presenter:**  
> "Watch what happens when I click **SCAN SCREEN**.  
> In under two seconds, SnapSafe AI captures the display and analyzes the frame.  
> It highlights every sensitive item with precision bounding boxes:  
> 🔴 **Critical:** The OpenAI API key and temporary password.  
> 🟠 **High:** The Student ID and emergency mobile number.  
> 🟡 **Medium:** The campus email.  
> Our on-device Privacy Coach immediately explains the exact risk and recommends instant masking."

---

### [00:50 – 01:10] The Transformation (Before / After)
*(Presenter clicks **[ PROCEED TO SAFE SHARE ]** and drags the interactive comparison slider.)*

> **Presenter:**  
> "Now, look at the magic of Safe Share.  
> With one click on **PROTECT & SHARE**, SnapSafe AI automatically redacts the sensitive bounding boxes using Gaussian blur or solid blackout.  
> Here is the interactive before-and-after slider:  
> Our Privacy Risk exposure plunges from **87 all the way down to 4**.  
> The screen is pristine, safe, and ready to broadcast."

> Sending confidential screenshots to a cloud AI endpoint is a severe privacy violation.

*(Presenter returns to the Dashboard showing 'Protection Active'.)*
